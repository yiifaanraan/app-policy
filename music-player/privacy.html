Privacy Policy

Local Music Player

Local Music Player (“the App”) is an offline, local music player. There is no in-app account system, and we do not run a server that collects your music library or personal profile.

Summary

  • No registration or login inside the App.
  • Your music, playlists, and settings stay on your device.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • Purchases (if any) are processed by Google Play / Apple App Store, not by us.

What Stays on Your Device

Depending on how you use the App, it may store locally: metadata of scanned music (title, artist, album, file path), playlists, favorites, queue, playback position, theme/preferences, optional custom cover images, and an optional local crash summary shown to you. None of this is uploaded to servers we operate.

Permissions

Requested only for their stated purpose, availability varies by OS version:

  • Media / storage — find and play the audio files you choose.
  • Photos — only when you pick a custom cover image.
  • Notifications / background playback — media controls and continued playback.
  • Audio settings — playback effects where supported.
  • Ignore battery optimizations (Android, optional) — fewer unexpected playback stops.
  • Display over other apps (Android, optional) — desktop/overlay lyrics only.
  • Bluetooth audio routing — device detection for car/Bluetooth audio, not contacts.

You can deny or revoke any permission in system settings; related features will be limited.

Purchases

Subscriptions or one-time unlocks are billed via Google Play / Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. We only receive the entitlement status needed to unlock features — never your full payment details.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information or share your local library with third parties for marketing. Limited data may reach Google/Apple as needed for distribution, payments, and OS services.

Retention & Deletion

Local data remains until you clear it in the App, clear app storage, or uninstall. Uninstalling removes the App's local database on that device. Store purchase records stay with Google/Apple; use "Restore purchases" after reinstall if your entitlement still applies.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account system, because none exists.

Changes

This policy may be updated occasionally; the date above will change accordingly. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
